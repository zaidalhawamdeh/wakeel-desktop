# Wakeel for Windows · وكيل للكمبيوتر

**Download / تنزيل:** [Wakeel-Setup.exe](https://github.com/zaidalhawamdeh/wakeel-desktop/releases/latest/download/Wakeel-Setup.exe)

The Wakeel dashboard ([wakeelhq.com](https://wakeelhq.com)) in its own window on the shop's computer:

- A new order is **printed by itself** on the shop's printer (80 mm or 58 mm roll, or A4).
- It **keeps ringing** until the order is accepted or rejected.
- It **starts with Windows** and keeps running next to the clock.

## التنصيب

1. نزّل الملف وافتحه.
2. ويندوز ممكن يطلّع «Windows protected your PC» — اكبس **More info** ثم **Run anyway**.
3. سجّل دخول بحساب متجرك على وكيل.
4. من الزر «وكيل على الجهاز» تحت: اختار الطابعة وعرض الورق، واكبس «طباعة تجريبية».

## Install

1. Download and open the file.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Sign in with your Wakeel store account.
4. Open "Wakeel on this PC" at the bottom, pick the printer and paper width, and press "Test print".

Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. Support: hello@wakeelhq.com
